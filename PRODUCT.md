## Platform

web

## Users

- *(inferred)* Followers of @PEoperator on X (and Substack readers) who tap a link, often on a
  phone, to see which books PE Operator recommends and pick one to buy or read next.
- *(inferred)* Operators, founders, and investors in or around private equity who want a
  trusted, opinionated shortlist rather than a bestseller list.

## Product Purpose

A personal, hand-picked book recommendation shelf: "Books that actually changed how I think."
Visitors browse the four Mt. Rushmore picks, six categories, the latest additions, or the full
A–Z list, and click a cover to buy on Amazon. Success = a visitor finds a book that fits them
and clicks through. *(inferred)*

## Positioning

The list is one operator's own reading, with his personal favorites marked by a lightning
bolt. It is curated and personal, not algorithmic or exhaustive. *(inferred)*

## Operating Context

- Static site generated by `scripts/build_site.py` from `scripts/extracted_books.json` and
  `scripts/categories.json`; deployed on Vercel from GitHub `PEoperator/book-recommendations`.
- Arrival is usually from X; a "Recommend a book" X intent button in the footer lets visitors
  suggest books back.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home IA order is fixed: Mt. Rushmore (4 books) → 6 category cards → Latest five → footer.
- Search: home is site-wide; category pages and View all are page-scoped.
- Favorites filter (All | Favorites) exists on category pages and View all, never on home.
  No persistence: resets on every load.
- Category cross-navigation on category pages and View all.
- Every Amazon affiliate link (tag `peoperator-20`, amzn.to and link.amazon shorts) must stay
  byte-identical. Affiliate disclosure stays in the footer.
- Content per book: title, author, cover, optional favorite (lightning) mark.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "PE Operator" / handle @PEoperator. Title copy "PE Operator's Book Recommendations"
  and tagline "Books that actually changed how I think."
- Banner art `Untitled.jpg` (illustrated avatar over charcoal with yellow, blue, and orange
  lightning bolts) and the round lightning badge `lightning.png` that marks favorites.
- Voice: plain, personal, a little playful ("Made post-workout by @PEoperator").

## Evidence on Hand

- 271 books, 79 favorites, 4 Mt. Rushmore picks, 6 categories (counts computed at build).
- No reviews, ratings, blurbs, or testimonials exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. The covers are the content; chrome stays out of their way.
2. Personal taste is the signal: favorites and Mt. Rushmore must be unmistakable.
3. Fast to scan on a phone, one tap to Amazon.
4. Never alter or break an affiliate link.
